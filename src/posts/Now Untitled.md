While you are sleeping, a shudder   
barely felt, deep inside the fault line,  
an unheard bass rumble of construction  
forgotten by morning. Before anyone notices,  
the quiet erasure of a country.

Maps redrawn, history books razor cut,  
roads that end in a sudden flux,  
borders that guard the smudged nothing.

'We're taking a stand,' a representative states  
'We can no longer stand idly by' -  
then silence. You sit at a notebook  
attempting to preserve what little  
you can remember. Renowned for pottery,  
a specific corn glaze, or was that  
some other peoples? Already, your knowledge  
becomes burning building smog,  
become a sandstorm, roaming and scattered.
